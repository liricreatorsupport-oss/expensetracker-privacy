ExpenseTracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

ExpenseTracker stores your expenses, categories, and settings only on your device. This information is used to display your expense history and statistics.

The app does not require an account, contains no advertising, and does not transmit your expense data to the developer or third parties.

Your information is kept in the app’s private Android storage. You can edit or delete your records. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may result in permanent data loss.
