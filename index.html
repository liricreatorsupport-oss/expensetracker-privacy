ExpenseTracker — Privacy policy

Updated: 19 November 2026.

Your data

Income, expenses, categories and settings stay on your device to provide history, statistics and balances. Financial records are not sent to the developer. No account, ads or tracking are used.

Currency conversion

Rates come from the ECB or through Frankfurter. These services receive connection data, such as your IP address, but not your financial records.

Deleting data

You can delete entries in the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove local records.

Backups

You create backups in a chosen folder; files are not encrypted. If you choose cloud storage, its provider stores the file. Exported backups remain after uninstalling.
