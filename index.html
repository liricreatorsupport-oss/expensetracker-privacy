ExpenseTracker — Privacy policy

Updated: 22 November 2026

Your data

The app stores your income, expenses, dates, currencies, categories, notes and settings only on your device. These are used for financial records, search and statistics. The developer does not collect your financial records. There are no accounts, ads or user tracking.

Internet

Exchange rates are obtained over a secure HTTPS connection from the ECB or Frankfurter. These services may receive your IP address and technical connection data, but not your financial records.

Security and deletion

Data is kept in private Android app storage until you delete it or uninstall the app. Manually exported backups are not encrypted. If you choose cloud storage, its provider stores the backup. Exported backups must be deleted separately.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the ExpenseTracker developer: liricreator.support@gmail.com
